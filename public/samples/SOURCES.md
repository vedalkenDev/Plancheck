# Sample drawing sources

PDF examples for the Plancheck UI. CAD files used in tests live in `fixtures/cad`, not here.

- `747-villa.pdf` — [747 villa Floor plan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:747_villa_Floor_plan.pdf) by MichaelScott99, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- `dogtrot-floorplan.pdf` — from [Typical Dogtrot Floorplan.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Typical_Dogtrot_Floorplan.svg) by Altairisfar / Matthew Woehlke, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- `woodshed-plan.pdf` — plotted from [xuminic/woodshed `plan.dxf`](https://github.com/xuminic/woodshed) (MIT).
- `marine-drive.pdf` / `hartley-test2.pdf` — plotted from the Plancheck CAD fixtures.
- `marine-drive-set.pdf` — Marine Drive notes split across two sheets.

Do not treat these as municipal submissions or as a SANS-compliant set.
